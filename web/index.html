ESP32-S3 Sensor Device

One control panel per sensor module. The device runs one at a time, chosen at build time (make build SENSOR=tof or SENSOR=ldc) -- open the panel matching what is flashed. See docs/17-web-control-panel.md.

VL53L1X Time-of-Flight

Distance, presence, and calibration for the M5Stack ToF4M Grove unit. Firmware: SENSOR=tof (the default).

Open Try with no hardware

LDC1612 2-Channel Inductive

Raw count, frequency, and presence for the Seeed Grove inductive sensor. Firmware: SENSOR=ldc.

Open Try with no hardware
